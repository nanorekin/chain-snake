How to play

Goal

Steer the chain to collect coins. Each coin adds a new link, and every link shows the ticker of a different crypto. Don't hit the walls (in Classic) or your own chain.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD, swipes on the board, or the on-screen pad on phones. Space or P pauses. The game pauses by itself if you switch tabs.

Modes

Classic: walls are deadly. Wrap: you come out the other side, so only your own chain can stop you. Daily: walls are deadly and the coins appear in the same order for everyone (00:00 UTC reset). Collect 20 coins to clear it and keep your daily streak.

Nano bonus coin

A glowing XNO coin appears every so often and fades after 8 seconds. It is worth 3 coins and gives you 4 seconds of slow motion, because Nano is feeless and fast.

Bull Run

Very rarely, once your chain is 10 links or longer, a green BULL coin appears for 10 seconds. Catch it and your chain shrinks by up to 5 links (never below 3) and you get +2 coins. It does not change the daily coin order.

Speed

The chain starts at a relaxed pace and speeds up as you collect coins, up to a limit.

Ranks

Coins collected in one run earn a rank: Paper Hands (0), Hodler (8), Diamond Hands (16), Whale (25), Satoshi (40).

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the Gold, Neon and Mint chain skins. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
